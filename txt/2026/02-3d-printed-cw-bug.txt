A 3D printed CW bug

A 3D printable semi-automatic Morse key (a "bug") for ham radio
operators. It has no springs or bearings: printed flexures act as both
the pivot and the spring. A magnet on the pendulum closes a single
reed switch for both dits and dahs.
<a href="https://github.com/kbsriram/3d-printed-cw-bug">Source and STL files</a>,
and a <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0ebGyvmV2f0">video of it working</a>.
